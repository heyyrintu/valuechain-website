## Why "integrated" matters

Most supply chains are stitched together from separate vendors — one for warehousing, another for packaging, a third for distribution. Every handover between them adds cost, delay and risk.

Drona Valuechain was built to remove those handovers. One partner, one point of accountability, one view of your supply chain.
