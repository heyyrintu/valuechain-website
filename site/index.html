The OEM challenge

Today's OEM supply chain is fragmented. Nobody owns the whole flow.

01
Too many handovers Storage, transport, returns and data each sit with a different vendor. Every handover adds cost, delay and risk.
02
No view below Tier-1 Plants depend on hundreds of Tier-2 and Tier-3 suppliers, with little visibility into what moves and when.
03
Costly disruptions Line stoppages from late parts, excess safety stock, part-filled trucks and slow, manual reporting across vendors.
Drona Valuechain exists to own that flow, end to end.

FAQ

What people ask us.

Can't find what you need? Write to info@dronavaluechain.com.

What does Drona Valuechain do?+

We build integrated warehousing and logistics clusters for India's OEMs and their supplier networks, and run warehousing, transportation, 3PL and 4PL for them on our own technology.

Where are you based?+

Our office is at Pioneer Urban Square, Gurugram, Haryana. Operations begin in November 2026 in our launch corridor.

Which sectors do you focus on?+

Automotive and auto components, consumer durables, electronics, and quick commerce / D2C.

What is the difference between 3PL and 4PL?+

In 3PL we execute your warehousing and transportation under one contract. In 4PL we orchestrate your entire logistics network, including other vendors, through a single control tower.

Can we start with just one service?+

Yes. Clients usually start with one lane, site or service as a pilot, then add more as the service proves itself.

How does an engagement start?+

With a discovery call and a site visit to walk through your current flows. We then design the network, process and service levels, and propose a pilot.
